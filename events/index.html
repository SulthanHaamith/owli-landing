About the Event

Arokya Payanam 2026 is a one-day health and wellness awareness programme & conference designed to encourage healthier living through practical knowledge, lifestyle awareness, Yoga and Naturopathy.

The programme brings together health professionals, students, wellness professionals, business communities and the general public through expert sessions and interactive activities.

The event will feature six expert speaker sessions of 45 minutes each, along with three independently conducted competitions.

6Expert Speaker Sessions
3Competitions
45Minutes Per Session
350+Expected Participants

Event Objectives

  • Create awareness about healthy living and wellbeing
  • Encourage positive lifestyle changes
  • Promote prevention-oriented health practices
  • Create awareness about mental wellness and stress management
  • Introduce the practical importance of Yoga in daily life
  • Create awareness about the principles of Naturopathy
  • Connect health professionals, students and the general public
  • Encourage creativity and health awareness through competitions

Who Will Attend

Arokya Payanam 2026 is expected to bring together a diverse audience from healthcare, education, business and the general community.

200+Naturopathy Students
100+Doctors & Healthcare Professionals
50+BNI Members & Business Community

General Public

Health-conscious individuals, families and people interested in healthy living, Yoga, Naturopathy and preventive wellness.

Expected total footfall: 350+ participants, in addition to the general public.

Competitions

Arokya Payanam 2026 will feature three competitions.

Reels Competition

Fireless Cooking Competition

Poster Presentation

Competition Arrangement

  • All competitions will be conducted separately from the main speaker programme.
  • Dedicated competition areas, coordinators and judges will be assigned.
  • Competition activities will not interrupt or interfere with the 45-minute speaker sessions.
  • Each competition will also be organised independently to avoid interference between competitions.

Prize Distribution

Winners of the Reels Competition, Fireless Cooking Competition and Poster Presentation will be recognised on the main stage from 3:15 P.M. to 3:35 P.M.
