About Our Center

At Owl I Health Care Centre, we believe in the body's innate ability to heal itself. Our approach combines time-tested naturopathy traditions with modern wellness practices to deliver drug-free, holistic healing. Every treatment plan is personalized to address the root cause — not just the symptoms — supporting your wellness for body, mind, and soul.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about visiting Owl I Health Care Centre

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended for consultation and treatments. Walk-ins may be accommodated depending on availability.

How long is a consultation?

A consultation usually takes around 20–30 minutes, depending on the patient's condition and requirements.

What should I bring for my first visit?

Please bring your previous medical reports, prescriptions, investigation reports, and details of any medicines or treatments you are currently taking.

Can I continue my existing medicines?

Yes. Do not stop or change prescribed medicines on your own. Inform our doctor about your current medicines so that your care can be planned appropriately.

How many treatment sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies depending on your condition, treatment plan and progress. The doctor will recommend a suitable schedule after assessment.

Can children and elderly patients undergo treatment?

Yes. Treatment can be considered for children and elderly patients after assessing their age, health condition and individual requirements.

Can I attend yoga without a consultation?

Yes, for general yoga classes. However, yoga therapy for a specific health condition may require a consultation and assessment first.

Can I come only for a specific therapy?

Yes, depending on the therapy. For therapeutic procedures, an assessment may be required to ensure that the treatment is suitable for you.

Can I visit Owl I Health Care Centre for wellness even if I have no illness?

Yes. You can visit for preventive health, wellness, lifestyle improvement, yoga, nutrition and healthy living guidance.

Is every treatment suitable for everyone?

No. Treatments are selected according to the individual's health condition, age, requirements and contraindications.
